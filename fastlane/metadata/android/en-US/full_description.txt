Stat Up - RPG Gamification for Real Life

Stat Up isn't just a to-do app. It turns real-life productivity into character progression.

Do tasks, earn points, improve your stats, hold your streak, climb the ranks, and spend what you earn on rewards you set yourself. Everything runs on your device.

FEATURES:
• RPG stats system - Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, Charisma, Vitality
• Hexagon radar chart with tap-to-expand stat details
• Rank system - progress from E → S based on streak consistency
• Create custom tasks with priority and stat assignment
• Custom rewards to redeem with earned points
• Achievement system with built-in and custom achievements
• Streak calendar with month navigation
• Home-screen widget - rank, balance, streak, and today's earned points at a glance
• Real GPU backdrop blur on Android 12+ (auto-falls-back on older devices)
• No ads, no tracking, no built-in data collection

OPTIONAL INTEGRATIONS (your data stays yours, opt-in only):
• Todoist - connect your account to earn points for completing tasks; supports label-to-stat routing
• AI Coach - bring your own Google Gemini API key for a private chat coach that talks to you about your stats and progress (chat history is not stored)

PRIVACY:
• Offline-first core - stats, tasks, rewards, achievements, history, and mood check-ins never leave your device
• Local-only storage by default; Todoist and Gemini run only if you provide your own credentials
• API tokens and keys stored encrypted on-device (AES-256-GCM)
• No analytics, crash reporting, advertising IDs, or third-party tracking
• Open source - review the code yourself

PERFECT FOR:
• Personal motivation and goal tracking
• Habit building and daily routines
• Gamifying personal achievements
• Privacy-conscious users who want local-by-default data storage

This app is completely free and open source (GPL-3.0). Source code available for review and contributions.
