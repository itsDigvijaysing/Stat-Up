Ranks now run on work days, and there's a new EX rank at the top.

• Any day you earn something is a work day. Work days plus your average stat carry you from E to EX
• Work days never reset when you rank up
• Miss a day and you lose one work day and a point off your highest stat - not all six
• Stats level twice as fast: 5 points per stat point
• New tasks get sorted into a stat for you, on your device
• A guided tour on first run, plus a How It Works page
